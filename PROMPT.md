"I am building a modern Developer Conference 2026 website using only HTML and CSS. I have already Navbar, Banner, Meet the Speakers, Secure Your Spot (Pricing) section. Give me another section design idea which is Venue section. The section design must be relevant to the DevConf 2026 theme. My website primary color is: White and #1D4ED8."

DeepSeek AI provided the initial concept and layout idea. The final UI, HTML structure, CSS styling and overall implementation were designed and coded entirely by me.
